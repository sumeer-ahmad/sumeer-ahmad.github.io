More Space, Less Privacy? IP-based Website Fingerprinting in IPv6

A plain-language write-up of my 2025 Pulse fellowship study. An observer who only sees IP addresses can tell which website you visit about 94% of the time when the site still relies on IPv4-only resources, and about 45% of the time for sites that run fully over IPv6. What matters is how hosting providers deploy IPv6.
