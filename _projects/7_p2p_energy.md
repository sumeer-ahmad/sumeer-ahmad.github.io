A prototype market where homes trade electricity directly with each other.

- Led the development of a blockchain based peer to peer energy trading system on a smart grid testbed with two producers, two consumers and two prosumers.
- Compared IoT focused blockchains such as Quorum, IoTeX and Helium on scalability, energy efficiency, consensus, and transaction throughput.
- Designed double auction smart contracts and token mechanisms for trading, inflation, generation and decay.

LUMS Energy Institute.
