Residential proxy services route customer traffic through ordinary home devices.
This project studied what that traffic looks like and how it gets abused.

- Analyzed the network flows of 10 residential proxy services.
- Deployed more than 25 proxy nodes and 3 honeypots, and used deep packet inspection to detect misuse such as botnets, credential stuffing and ad fraud.
- Used LLMs to classify traffic automatically by malicious activity, encryption, anomalies and evasion tactics.
- Measured the bandwidth and compute cost that proxy participation puts on the device owner.

Hexlab, Stony Brook University.
