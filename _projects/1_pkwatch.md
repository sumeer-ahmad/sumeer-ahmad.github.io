Pakistan blocks a large and changing set of websites, but public evidence on what is blocked, and how, is scattered.
PKWatch measures this systematically.

**What we do**

- Test more than 10 million domains from two vantage points, one in Pakistan and one in the US, and compare the results.
- Identify the blocking methods in use: DNS injection, TCP resets and SNI based filtering.
- Run targeted experiments that tell stateless filtering apart from stateful deep packet inspection.
- Characterize blocking in both directions and across protocols: HTTP, TLS, encrypted DNS (DoH and DoT), ECH and QUIC (HTTP/3).
- Evaluate which circumvention strategies get past these filters.

This work is done with the [Systopia lab](https://systopia.cs.ubc.ca/) at UBC. A paper is under review.
